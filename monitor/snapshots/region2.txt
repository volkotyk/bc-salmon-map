Region 2 - Lower Mainland: Recreational salmon fishing limits, openings and closures
You can only fish for salmon in Region 2 during daylight hours.
No fishing is allowed within 100 meters of any government facility operated for counting, passing or rearing fish.
You must be aware of these possession limits:
The daily limit for all species of Pacific salmon from tidal and fresh waters combined is 4. Individual species limits also apply.
The possession limit for all salmon from all waters is twice the daily limit. You cannot possess more than 8 salmon in total, except for salmon that are at your ordinary residence.
The total annual limit for chinook salmon over 50 cm is 10 fish from all fresh waters combined.
Squamish watershed: the aggregate daily limit of hatchery-marked coho in the entire Squamish non-tidal systems combined is one (1).
Salmon measurements are taken from tip of nose to tail fork. You must be aware of these measurements:
All retained coho must measure 25 cm or more.
All retained chinook, chum, pink, and sockeye must measure 30 cm or more.
An "adult chinook" in Region 2 is defined as being over 50 cm except in the following areas where an "adult chinook" is defined as being over 62 cm:
the Fraser River from Sep 1 to Dec 31
the Chehalis River from Sep 1 to Dec 31
the Chilliwack/Vedder River (including Sumas River) and the Capilano River.

Salmon limits, openings and closures
There is no fishing for salmon in waters of Region 2 flowing into Areas 28 and 29 except for the opportunities listed in the table below.
Unless otherwise stated in the table below, the daily limit for waters in Region 2 flowing into Areas 13, 15, and 16 is zero (0).
This table refers to fishing for salmon only. For information on other species please refer to the B.C. Freshwater Fishing Regulations Synopsis [https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content?id=473C6B6D0B21471EA5D9C760BF4009C4].
