Salmon
You must have a valid a salmon conservation stamp [/fm-gp/rec/licence-permis/salmon-stamp-timbre-saumon-eng.html] on your BC Tidal Waters sport fishing licence if you plan to retain salmon.
You must immediately and permanently record all retained chinook salmon on your paper licence, your NRLS catch log [https://recfish-pechesportive.dfo-mpo.gc.ca/nrls-sndpp/index-eng.cfm] or your NRLS-linked FishingBC app catch log [/fm-gp/rec/licence-permis/fishingbc-pechecb-app-faq-eng.html#catch-reporting].

In tidal waters, it is illegal to angle with a fishing line that has more than one hook, artificial lure or artificial fly attached, with the following exceptions: (1) When bait fishing, you may attach multiple hooks to hold a single piece of bait as long as the hooks are not arranged to catch more than one fish. Reminder |
Keeping your chinook salmon? You must permanently record all retained chinook salmon (head-on length) on your Tidal Waters Sport Fishing Licence. The licence holder may alternatively record retained catch immediately in an electronic licence held in an approved third-party application linked to their National Recreational Licensing System (NRLS) account, or if mobile internet access is immediately available, directly in their NRLS account.
